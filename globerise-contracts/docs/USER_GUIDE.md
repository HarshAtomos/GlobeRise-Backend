## Welcome to GlobeRise

GlobeRise is a decentralized MLM (Multi-Level Marketing) investment platform built on the Ethereum blockchain. The platform offers multiple income streams including:

- 💰 **Monthly ROI** (8%-12% monthly returns - tier based on your referrals!)
- 📦 **Staking Packages** (1.25%-4.75% monthly, guaranteed returns)
- 🤝 **Direct Referral Commissions** (5% instant)
- 📊 **Level Income** (Up to 16 levels, 10%-1%)
- 👑 **Royalty Income** (Monthly platform revenue sharing)
- 🎁 **Rank Bonuses** (One-time rewards from $250 to $50,000)

### Key Features

✅ **Decentralized** - Built on Ethereum, secured by smart contracts
✅ **Transparent** - All transactions visible on blockchain
✅ **Upgradeable** - Platform can be improved without losing data
✅ **Secure** - Multiple code reviews, comprehensive testing
✅ **Fair** - 60:40 binary tree qualification ensures balanced growth

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before using GlobeRise, you'll need:

1. **A Web3 Wallet**
   - MetaMask (recommended)
   - Trust Wallet
   - Binance Wallet
   - Or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet

2. **Ethereum (ETH)** for gas fees
   - Recommended: 0.05-0.1 ETH for multiple transactions
   - Gas fees vary based on network congestion

3. **GRT Tokens** (GlobeRise Token)
   - Can be purchased with USDT, ETH, or other cryptos
   - Minimum investment: 100 GRT (≈$100)

### Step 1: Connect Your Wallet

1. Visit the GlobeRise platform website
2. Click "Connect Wallet" in the top right
3. Select your wallet type (MetaMask, Trust Wallet, etc.)
4. Approve the connection in your wallet
5. Your wallet address will appear in the top right

### Step 2: Register Your Account

1. Click "Register" or "Join GlobeRise"
2. If you have a referral code, enter it in the "Sponsor Code" field
   - ⚠️ **Important:** This cannot be changed later!
   - ⚠️ **Note:** If sponsor is inactive for 90+ days, choose another sponsor
   - If no referral code, leave blank (you'll be placed under platform)
3. Click "Complete Registration"
4. Confirm the transaction in your wallet
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation (1-2 minutes)
6. You'll see a success message with your unique referral code

**Gas Cost:** ~$5-15 USD (varies with network)

### Step 3: Get GRT Tokens

**Option A: Buy GRT directly (if available on exchanges)**
1. Purchase GRT from supported exchanges
2. Send to your wallet address
3. Verify balance in your wallet

**Option B: Swap for GRT using the platform**
1. Go to "Buy GRT" section
2. Choose input currency (USDT, ETH, BTC, etc.)
3. Enter amount
4. Review exchange rate and fees
5. Confirm swap
6. GRT will arrive in your wallet

## Making Investments

### Dynamic ROI System

**⚠️ NEW in v2.0:** ROI tier is no longer fixed at investment time. Your tier is calculated **dynamically at each claim** based on your referral activity!

| Tier | Monthly ROI | Max Returns | How to Qualify |
|------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Tier 1** | 8% | 2.5X (250%) | Default (no requirements) |
| **Tier 2** | 10% | 3X (300%) | 2+ referrals in 14 days who invested same/more |
| **Tier 3** | 12% | 4X (400%) | 4+ referrals in 21 days who invested same/more |

**Important:** Your referrals must:
1. Join within 14 days (Tier 2) or 21 days (Tier 3) of your investment
2. Invest **the same amount or more** than your investment

### How to Create an Investment

1. **Navigate to "Invest" section**
   - Click "New Investment" or "Invest Now"

2. **Enter Investment Amount**
   - Minimum: 100 GRT
   - Enter desired amount (e.g., 500 GRT)
   - Platform will show expected monthly ROI (starts at 8%)

3. **Approve Token Spending**
   - Click "Approve GRT"
   - This allows the platform to access your GRT
   - Confirm in your wallet
   - **Gas Cost:** ~$3-8 USD
   - Wait for confirmation

4. **Confirm Investment**
   - Click "Confirm Investment"
   - Review details one final time
   - Confirm transaction in your wallet
   - **Gas Cost:** ~$15-30 USD
   - Wait for blockchain confirmation (1-2 minutes)

5. **Success!**
   - You'll see investment details
   - ROI starts accruing immediately (at 8% base rate)
   - Your sponsor receives 5% commission instantly
   - **Build referrals within 14-21 days to unlock higher tiers!**

### Investment Example

**Scenario:** You invest 1,000 GRT

**Without qualifying referrals (Tier 1):**
- Monthly ROI: 8% = 80 GRT/month
- Max Returns: 2.5X = 2,500 GRT total

**With 2 qualifying referrals in 14 days (Tier 2):**
- Monthly ROI: 10% = 100 GRT/month
- Max Returns: 3X = 3,000 GRT total

**With 4 qualifying referrals in 21 days (Tier 3):**
- Monthly ROI: 12% = 120 GRT/month
- Max Returns: 4X = 4,000 GRT total

## Understanding Income Types

### 1. ROI Income (Monthly Returns)

**What it is:** Monthly returns on your investment based on your dynamic tier.

**How it works:**
- ROI accrues automatically every month (30 days)
- Tier is calculated at each claim based on qualifying referrals
- You can claim anytime after 30 days from investment
- Only **passive ROI** counts toward your cap

**Cap Info:** The cap (2.5X, 3X, or 4X) only applies to passive ROI. Bonuses and commissions are **uncapped**.

### 2. Direct Referral Commission (5%)

**What it is:** Instant 5% bonus when your direct referral invests.

**How it works:**
- Someone joins using your referral code
- They make an investment
- You instantly receive 5% in your withdrawable balance
- **No limit on referrals!** (was 16, now unlimited)

**Example:**
```
Your referral invests: 1,000 GRT
You receive: 50 GRT (5%)
Available to withdraw: Immediately
```

### 3. Level Income (16 Levels)

**What it is:** Passive income from your downline's ROI claims across 16 levels.

**Level Income Rates:**

| Level | Your Share | Example (on $100 ROI claim) |
|-------|------------|------------------------------|
| Level 1 | 10% | $10 |
| Level 2 | 5% | $5 |
| Level 3-4 | 4% each | $4 each |
| Level 5-7 | 3% each | $3 each |
| Level 8-11 | 2% each | $2 each |
| Level 12-16 | 1% each | $1 each |

### 4. Royalty Income (Monthly CTO Sharing)

**What it is:** Share of the company's monthly turnover (CTO) based on your rank.

**Qualification (Updated!):**
- Generate **10% growth** from previous month's team business
- Example: Last month $50,000 → This month must be $55,000+
- Maintain your rank qualifications

### 5. Rank Bonuses (One-Time Rewards)

**What it is:** One-time cash bonus when you achieve a new rank.

## Withdrawing Your Earnings

### ⚠️ IMPORTANT: Monday-Only Withdrawals!

You can only request withdrawals on **Mondays (UTC)**. Plan accordingly!

### Withdrawal Options

1. **100% GRT** - Receive full amount in GRT tokens
2. **50/50 Split** - Receive 50% GRT + 50% USDT

### Withdrawal Fee

A **10% platform fee** is deducted from all withdrawals:
- 2.5% → Development & Gas costs
- 7.5% → Platform treasury

**Example:**
```
You withdraw: 100 GRT
Platform fee (10%): 10 GRT
You receive: 90 GRT
```

### Withdrawal Process

#### Step 1: Request Withdrawal (Monday Only!)

1. **Check if it's Monday (UTC)**
2. **Go to "Withdraw" section**
3. **Check "Available Balance"**
4. **Enter Amount** (minimum: 10 GRT)
5. **Select Withdrawal Type** (100% GRT or 50/50)
6. **Click "Request Withdrawal"**
   - Confirm transaction
   - **Gas Cost:** ~$10-20 USD

#### Step 2: Admin Approval

- Admin reviews your withdrawal request
- Typically approved within 24-48 hours
- Status changes to: APPROVED

#### Step 3: Operator Processes

- Operator completes the transfer
- **10% fee is deducted**
- Net tokens sent to your wallet
- Status changes to: COMPLETED

### Withdrawal Timeline

| Day | Can You Request? | Notes |
|-----|-----------------|-------|
| Monday | ✅ YES | Withdrawal day! |
| Tuesday-Sunday | ❌ NO | Wait for Monday |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Investment Questions

**Q: How does the dynamic ROI tier work?**
A: Your tier is calculated at each claim based on how many qualifying referrals you have. Build referrals quickly to unlock higher tiers!

**Q: What qualifies a referral for tier upgrades?**
A: They must:
1. Join within 14 days (Tier 2) or 21 days (Tier 3) of your investment
2. Invest the same amount or more than you did

**Q: Can my tier decrease?**
A: No. Once you qualify for a higher tier, you keep it for that investment.

### Staking Questions

**Q: Is staking different from investing?**
A: Yes! Staking is a separate product with guaranteed returns. It does NOT count towards MLM volumes or commissions.

**Q: Can I withdraw my stake early?**
A: No. Staking has a lock period. You must wait for maturity.

### Withdrawal Questions

**Q: Why can I only withdraw on Mondays?**
A: This is a platform rule to manage liquidity and processing.

**Q: What's the 10% fee for?**
A: 2.5% covers gas and development costs, 7.5% goes to platform treasury.

### Dormancy Questions

**Q: What makes me dormant?**
A: 90 days without any activity (investment, ROI claim, or withdrawal request).

**Q: How do I reactivate my account?**
A: Make any transaction: invest, claim ROI, or request withdrawal.

## Troubleshooting

### "NotWithdrawalDay" Error

**Problem:** "Withdrawal not allowed today"

**Solution:** Wait until Monday (UTC) to request withdrawals.

### "SponsorDormant" Error

**Problem:** Cannot register under chosen sponsor

**Solution:** Choose a different sponsor who is active. The sponsor you selected hasn't been active for 90+ days.

### "StakeNotMature" Error

**Problem:** Cannot claim stake

**Solution:** Wait until the maturity date shown in your stake details.

### Cannot Request Withdrawal

**Problem:** Button greyed out

**Solutions:**
1. Check if today is Monday (UTC)
2. Check your withdrawable balance
3. Ensure minimum 10 GRT
